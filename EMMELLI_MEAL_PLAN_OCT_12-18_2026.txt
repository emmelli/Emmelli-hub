RECIPE 1 — CREAMY CHICKEN-BACON PASTA + CAULIFLOWER
Makes 2 portions. Cook Monday. Eat Monday dinner and Tuesday dinner.

Ingredients:
454 g raw chicken breast, cubed
30 g cooked bacon, chopped
100 g dry pasta
240 ml Fairlife whole ultra-filtered milk
40 g cream cheese
150 g frozen spinach, thawed and squeezed
100 g onion, diced
15 g minced garlic
400 g frozen cauliflower for the side
Salt, black pepper, garlic/onion seasoning as desired

Method:
1. Heat oven/air fryer to 425°F. Season cauliflower and roast 25–30 minutes.
2. Boil pasta. Reserve about 1/2 cup pasta water, then drain.
3. Cook onion and chicken in a large skillet until the chicken reaches 165°F. Add garlic for the final minute.
4. Lower heat. Stir in milk, cream cheese, spinach, bacon, and a little pasta water until glossy and saucy. Add pasta. Do not cook it dry.
5. Divide into 2 pasta portions and add 200 g cauliflower to each container.
Estimated per complete portion: about 780 calories, 75–80 g protein, about 60–65 g carbs, about 25 g fat, about 8 g fiber.

RECIPE 2 — MAPLE-DIJON PORK + SWEET POTATO + CARROTS
Makes 2 portions. Cook Monday. Eat Tuesday lunch and Thursday lunch.

Ingredients:
454 g pork tenderloin
500 g sweet potato, cubed
250 g carrots, sliced
100 g onion, sliced
30 g honey
20 g Dijon mustard
10 g oil
10 g minced garlic
Salt, pepper, smoked paprika if desired but no hot spice

Method:
1. Heat oven to 425°F. Toss sweet potato, carrots, and onion with most of the oil and seasoning. Roast 30–35 minutes.
2. Mix honey, Dijon, garlic, and remaining oil. Brush over pork.
3. Roast pork until it reaches 145°F, then rest at least 3 minutes before slicing.
4. Divide pork and all roasted vegetables evenly into 2 containers; spoon the pan juices over the pork so it stays moist.
Estimated per portion: about 660 calories, about 55–60 g protein, about 80–85 g carbs, about 14 g fat, about 12 g fiber.

RECIPE 3 — MILD HONEY-SOY STEAK RICE NOODLES + BROCCOLI/CARROTS
Makes 2 portions. Cook Wednesday. Eat Wednesday lunch and Friday dinner.

Ingredients:
360 g steak, thinly sliced
110 g dry rice noodles
600 g broccoli
150 g carrots
45 g low-sodium soy sauce or coconut aminos
25 g honey
5 g oil
15 g minced garlic
Black pepper; no hot sauce

Method:
1. Mix soy sauce, honey, garlic, and 3–4 tablespoons water.
2. Cook rice noodles according to package directions; rinse briefly and drain.
3. Sear steak quickly in oil and remove while still juicy.
4. Cook broccoli and carrots with a splash of water until tender-crisp.
5. Return steak, add noodles and sauce, and toss only until coated. Add another splash of water if needed so the noodles are not dry.
6. Divide evenly into 2 containers.
Estimated per portion: about 720 calories, about 54 g protein, about 85–90 g carbs, about 20 g fat, about 11 g fiber.

RECIPE 4 — CREAMY GARLIC CHICKEN RICE + SPINACH
Makes 2 portions. Cook Wednesday. Eat Wednesday dinner and Saturday lunch.

Ingredients:
454 g raw chicken breast
110 g dry basmati rice
150 g frozen spinach, thawed and squeezed
240 ml Fairlife whole ultra-filtered milk
240 ml chicken broth
40 g cream cheese
40 g mozzarella
100 g onion
15 g minced garlic
3 g oil
Seasoning to taste

Method:
1. Cook rice separately.
2. Cook onion and chicken in oil until chicken reaches 165°F. Add garlic for the final minute.
3. Lower heat. Add broth, milk, cream cheese, mozzarella, and spinach. Simmer gently until creamy; do not reduce it dry.
4. Divide rice between 2 containers and cover with chicken plus plenty of sauce.
Estimated per portion: about 750 calories, about 70–75 g protein, about 60 g carbs, about 23 g fat, about 3 g fiber.

RECIPE 5 — HONEY-TERIYAKI CHICKEN RICE + BROCCOLI
Makes 2 portions. Cook Thursday. Eat Thursday dinner and Saturday dinner.

Ingredients:
454 g raw boneless skinless chicken thighs
110 g dry basmati rice
400 g broccoli
45 g low-sodium soy sauce or coconut aminos
30 g honey
12 g cornstarch
15 g minced garlic
3 g oil
About 180 ml water

Method:
1. Cook rice separately.
2. Mix soy sauce, honey, garlic, cornstarch, and water.
3. Cook chicken in oil until it reaches 165°F. Steam/sauté broccoli until tender-crisp.
4. Add sauce and simmer until glossy but still loose enough to coat the rice.
5. Divide rice, chicken, broccoli, and every bit of sauce evenly into 2 containers.
Estimated per portion: about 700 calories, about 55 g protein, about 80 g carbs, about 19 g fat, about 6 g fiber.

RECIPE 6 — FRENCH ONION CHICKEN + SWEET POTATO/CAULIFLOWER
Makes 2 plated portions plus the plain chicken reserved for Sunday tacos. Cook Thursday. Eat the plated portions Friday lunch and Sunday lunch.

Ingredients:
614 g raw chicken breast total
300 g onion, thinly sliced
300 ml chicken broth
40 g mozzarella
400 g sweet potato, cubed
400 g frozen cauliflower
10 g butter
15 g minced garlic
Salt, black pepper, thyme

Method:
1. Before saucing, set aside 160 g of the raw chicken. Cook that piece plainly to 165°F, cool promptly, and refrigerate it for Sunday’s tacos. It should yield roughly 120 g cooked.
2. Heat oven to 425°F. Roast sweet potato and cauliflower 30–35 minutes.
3. Slowly soften onions in butter, add garlic, then add 300 ml broth.
4. Add the remaining 454 g chicken and cook to 165°F. Top with mozzarella. Keep enough onion broth in the pan for both servings.
5. Divide the sauced chicken and vegetables evenly into 2 containers. Store the plain taco chicken separately.
Estimated per plated portion: about 670 calories, about 65–70 g protein, about 65–70 g carbs, about 15 g fat, about 13 g fiber.
